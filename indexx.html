Frequently asked

Answers to the things you'd ask.

How is the audio quality compared to other AI tools?
Sonus outputs 48 kHz lossless audio. Most browser-based competitors downsample to 16–24 kHz to save on processing. The difference is audible — especially on cymbals, sibilants, and reverb tails. We don't compress what we don't have to.
Can I use Sonus offline or with sensitive material?
Yes. Studio and Enterprise plans include our offline desktop client, which runs all models locally on your machine. Nothing touches our servers, ever. We built this for labels and producers working with unreleased material.
Does it integrate with my DAW?
Sonus ships native plugins in VST3, AU, and AAX formats. They work in Pro Tools, Logic, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper, and any DAW that supports those plugin formats. You stay in your session — no exporting and re-importing.
What happens if I exceed my monthly hours?
Nothing breaks. You'll get a notification when you hit 80% of your cap, and you can either upgrade your plan or buy additional credits at €0.015/minute. We don't auto-charge you or block your work mid-render.
Is stem separation legal? What about copyright?
Stem separation for personal use, study, or transformative work is legal in most jurisdictions, including the EU and US. Distributing extracted stems commercially without rights is not — and that's on you. We watermark outputs and refuse processing of certain protected catalogs in regions with active enforcement.
How does Sonus actually compare to Moises or LALAL.AI?
In short: Moises is more affordable but caps quality at 16–24 kHz with no DAW plugins. LALAL.AI charges per-minute, which gets expensive fast. iZotope is excellent but costs €399+ as a one-shot desktop install. Sonus sits between them: pro-grade quality, predictable monthly pricing, native DAW integration. See the full comparison above.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel anytime, no questions asked. Annual plans (which save you 27%) can be cancelled at the end of the billing year. We'll never lock you in or hide the cancel button.
Do you offer discounts for students or educators?
Yes. Verified students get Pro at 50% off (€4.50/month). Music schools and conservatoires get custom Enterprise terms. Reach out via the contact form below with proof of enrollment or institution.
